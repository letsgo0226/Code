FORMAL TOTAL TURING MACHINE PROTOTYPE
Compact fair-diagonal ATGC reversible TM enumerator

1. Axiom
   Solution(s*) = s* under the Cosmic Love encoding.

2. Encoding
   E encodes two bit-strings into an ATGC string (reversible bit-pair map).

3. Machine generation
   machines(n) enumerates all n-state binary TMs structurally
   (states 0..n-1, -1=HALT; each (q,a) may be undefined or (q',b,L/R)).

4. Execution
   step advances one configuration; run detects FIX (fixed point),
   CLOSED (undefined transition), or OPEN (still running after k steps).

5. Fair diagonal schedule
   Round r visits state-count n<=r and the first r machines of each size
   for r steps. Counts of FIX/CLOSED/OPEN are reported; first FIX sample is recorded.

6. Scope
   Finite prototype of a fair enumeration; not a total solver of the halting problem.
   Formal model only. H = 0.
